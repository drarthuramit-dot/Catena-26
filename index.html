About the conference

Diagnostics that move at the speed of care.

CATENA 26 is an International Hybrid CME dedicated to the evolving role of Point-of-Care Testing (POCT) in modern healthcare. The meeting will bring together laboratory medicine, emergency care and clinical teams to discuss implementation, quality, workflow integration and the challenges of delivering reliable results at the point of care.

Designed for meaningful exchange between healthcare professionals, the programme will explore how POCT can support timely clinical decisions while maintaining analytical quality, patient safety and appropriate governance.
